# Question 17: Where does the product excel relative to competitors?

## Answer

SolarWinds Service Desk excels in **ease of use**, **rapid deployment**, **value-for-money pricing**, **HIPAA compliance with BAA**, and **unified ITSM/ITAM/ESM in a single platform**. The platform is particularly strong against tier-1 competitors on simplicity and cost, and against tier-2 competitors on compliance and enterprise readiness.

| Strength | Description | Competitive Advantage |
