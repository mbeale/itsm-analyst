# Question 13: What are typical deal cycles by segment?

## Answer

Deal cycles vary dramatically by segment: **SMB closes in 14-30 days**, **Mid-Market in 30-90 days**, and **Enterprise in 90-180+ days**. Cycles have lengthened 22% since 2022 due to larger buying committees and increased scrutiny. The median B2B SaaS cycle is now 84 days.

---

## Executive Summary

| Segment | ACV Range | Typical Cycle | Stakeholders | Key Characteristic |
|---------|-----------|---------------|--------------|-------------------|
| **SMB** | <$15K | 14-30 days | 1-3 | Single decision-maker, fast |
| **Mid-Market** | $15K-$100K | 30-90 days | 3-8 | Committee, moderate process |
| **Enterprise** | >$100K | 90-180+ days | 10-20+ | Formal RFP, security review |
| **Strategic** | >$500K | 6-18+ months | 15-30+ | Annual budget cycle dependent |

### Strategic Deals (>$500K)

| Metric | Benchmark |
|--------|-----------|
| **ACV Range** | >$500,000 |
| **Typical Cycle** | 6-18+ months |
| **Key Factor** | Annual budget cycle timing |

> "Deals greater than $500,000 in ACV are almost always budgeted annually. This means they can take from 6-18+ months to close. Shorter if you hit the annual budgeting process timing just right. Sometimes, a whole additional year if you don't."

## Sales Cycle Stages & Duration

### Typical B2B Sales Cycle Stages

| Stage | Description | % of Total Cycle |
|-------|-------------|------------------|
| **1. Prospecting** | Identify and reach potential buyers | 5-10% |
| **2. Qualification** | Determine fit and intent | 5-10% |
| **3. Discovery** | Understand needs, pain points, process | 10-15% |
| **4. Demo/Presentation** | Show solution tailored to needs | 10-15% |
| **5. POC/Trial** | Hands-on validation | 15-25% |
| **6. Proposal** | Formal pricing and scope | 5-10% |
| **7. Negotiation** | Terms, pricing, contract | 20-35% |
| **8. Close** | Final signatures, procurement | 5-15% |

### Stage Duration by Segment

| Stage | SMB | Mid-Market | Enterprise |
|-------|-----|------------|------------|
| **Prospecting/Qualification** | 1-3 days | 1-2 weeks | 2-4 weeks |
| **Discovery** | 1-2 days | 1-2 weeks | 2-4 weeks |
| **Demo** | 1 call | 1-2 calls | 3-5 calls (multiple audiences) |
| **POC/Trial** | 0-14 days | 2-4 weeks | 4-8 weeks |
| **Proposal** | Same day | 1 week | 2-4 weeks |
| **Negotiation** | 1-3 days | 2-4 weeks | 4-12 weeks |
| **Close** | Same day | 1-2 weeks | 2-8 weeks |

### Critical Insight: Where Deals Stall

> "The Negotiation → Close stage accounts for 35-40% of total cycle time in Enterprise deals. Legal redlines and procurement approval are the #1 cause of delayed closes."

| Stage | % of Deals That Stall | Primary Cause |
|-------|----------------------|---------------|
| Discovery | 15% | Poor qualification; no real need |
| POC | 20% | Technical issues; failed validation |
| Proposal | 10% | Budget surprise; scope mismatch |
| Negotiation | 35% | Legal, procurement, competing priorities |
| Close | 20% | Last-minute stakeholder objection |

## Factors That Lengthen Deal Cycles

### Market Trends (2022-2025)

| Trend | Impact |
|-------|--------|
| **Cycles lengthened 22%** | Budget scrutiny and committee buying |
| **Average stakeholders: 6.8** | Up from 5.4 in 2020 |
| **CFO involvement: +40%** | Finance now in most software deals |
| **Security questionnaires** | Standard even in mid-market (+2-4 weeks) |
| **Win rates: 19%** | Down from 23% in 2022 |

### Specific Factors That Slow Deals

| Factor | Impact on Cycle | Mitigation |
|--------|-----------------|------------|
| **Multiple stakeholders** | +30-60% longer | Multi-thread early; map committee |
| **Security review** | +4-8 weeks | Have SOC 2 report ready |
| **Legal review** | +2-6 weeks | Offer standard contract terms |
| **Competitive evaluation** | +4-8 weeks | Differentiate clearly; win early preference |
| **Budget timing** | +1-12 months | Align with fiscal year; find discretionary budget |
| **Organizational changes** | +4-12 weeks | Identify stable champions |
| **Lack of information** | +2-4 weeks | Provide comprehensive self-service materials |
| **Internal champion weakness** | +4-8 weeks | Enable champion with ROI data |

### The Budget Timing Factor

| Scenario | Impact |
|----------|--------|
| In-year discretionary budget | Can close quickly |
| Next year's budget planning | Wait 3-9 months |
| Missed budget cycle | Wait additional year |
| Emergency/compliance need | Bypasses normal cycle |

## Factors That Shorten Deal Cycles

### Acceleration Strategies

| Strategy | Impact | Evidence |
|----------|--------|----------|
| **Multi-threading** | 2.4x faster close | Only 9% of deals are multi-threaded |
| **Fast proposal delivery** | 35% faster | Send within 24 hours of demo |
| **Self-service demos** | Faster qualification | Buyers pre-educate before sales call |
| **Clear ROI documentation** | Faster approval | Enables champion to sell internally |
| **Trial/POC** | Reduces risk perception | Faster decision confidence |
| **Transparent pricing** | Shorter negotiation | No back-and-forth on pricing |
| **Standard contracts** | Shorter legal review | Pre-approved terms accelerate close |

### Situational Accelerators

| Situation | Why It Accelerates |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Urgent pain point** | Compliance deadline, outage, audit finding |
| **Executive mandate** | Top-down decision bypasses committee |
| **Incumbent failure** | Current tool failing creates urgency |
| **Contract expiration** | Deadline forces decision |
| **Expansion deal** | Existing relationship, less evaluation |
| **Pre-existing relationship** | Trust already established |

## Sales Velocity Formula

### The Formula

```
Sales Velocity = (# Opportunities × Deal Size × Win Rate) ÷ Cycle Length
```

### Example Impact

| Scenario | Opportunities | Deal Size | Win Rate | Cycle | Velocity/Month |
|----------|---------------|-----------|----------|-------|----------------|
| Baseline | 100 | $10,000 | 20% | 60 days | $100,000 |
| Faster cycle (30 days) | 100 | $10,000 | 20% | 30 days | $200,000 |
| Better win rate (30%) | 100 | $10,000 | 30% | 60 days | $150,000 |
| Larger deals ($15K) | 100 | $15,000 | 20% | 60 days | $150,000 |

**Insight:** Cutting cycle length in half doubles velocity — often easier than doubling win rate.

## Cycle Optimization Recommendations

### For SMB Deals

| Action | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| Offer self-service trial | Let buyers explore before calling |
| One-call close motion | Reduce touchpoints |
| Transparent pricing online | No proposal needed |
| Fast onboarding | Same-day activation |

### For Mid-Market Deals

| Action | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| Multi-thread from first call | Engage 3+ stakeholders early |
| Provide ROI calculator | Enable internal selling |
| Offer POC with success criteria | Accelerate validation |
| Have standard contract ready | Reduce legal delays |
| Send proposal within 24 hours | Maintain momentum |

### For Enterprise Deals

| Action | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| Map full buying committee | Know all stakeholders |
| Security package ready | SOC 2, pentest summary, architecture |
| Executive sponsor engagement | Top-down acceleration |
| Reference customers by vertical | Reduce perceived risk |
| Align with budget cycle | Timing is everything |
| Dedicated resources | Show commitment to large deals |
