# Question 9: What recent strategic moves have competitors made (acquisitions, partnerships, product launches)?

The ITSM competitive landscape has seen significant strategic activity in 2024-2025, with AI being the dominant theme across all major players. This document tracks key moves and their implications for SolarWinds Service Desk.

## Tier-1 Competitor Moves

### ServiceNow

#### Moveworks Acquisition (March 2025)

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Deal Value** | $2.85 billion (cash + stock) |
| **Closed** | December 15, 2025 |
| **Significance** | Largest acquisition in ServiceNow history |

**What Moveworks Brings:**
- Front-end AI assistant technology
- Enterprise search capabilities
- 5.5 million employee users
- 250+ joint customers already
- $100M+ ARR (as of Sept 2024)
- Customers: Hearst, Instacart, Siemens, Toyota, Unilever

**Strategic Rationale:**
> "The acquisition combines ServiceNow's agentic AI and automation strengths with Moveworks' front-end AI assistant and enterprise search technology to unlock new experiences for every employee."

**Key Insights:**
- ServiceNow explicitly targeting CRM market leadership (vs. Salesforce)
- 8-year partnership preceded acquisition
- Regulatory scrutiny (DOJ second request) delayed but didn't stop deal
- Part of broader AI acquisition spree

#### Other 2024-2025 Acquisitions

| Company | Focus Area | Timing |
|---------|------------|--------|
| **CueIn** | AI/Data | 2025 |
| **Logik.ai** | AI/Configuration | 2025 |
| **Quality360°** | Quality management | 2025 |
| **data.world** | Data catalog | 2025 |

**Pattern:** Aggressive "tuck-in" AI acquisition strategy (34th acquisition total)

#### AI Momentum

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| AI customers | ~1,000 |
| Pro Plus AI ACV | $200M+ (as of Dec 2024) |
| AI deal growth | 150% Q/Q |

**Implication for SolarWinds:** ServiceNow is creating an insurmountable AI moat at the enterprise level. Mid-market positioning becomes even more important.

### BMC Helix

#### Corporate Spin-Off (October 2024 - Early 2025)

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Announcement** | October 2024 |
| **Effective** | Early 2025 |
| **Structure** | Two independent companies |
| **Ownership** | Both remain under KKR |

**The Split:**
| Company | Focus | Website |
|---------|-------|---------|
| **BMC** | Mainframe (IZOT), Digital Business Automation (DBA) | bmc.com |
| **BMC Helix** | Digital Service and Operations Management (DSOM) | helixops.ai |

**Strategic Rationale:**
- Accelerate innovation in each segment
- Enable "agility to innovate at the pace demanded by AI"
- Better address distinct market demands
- Focus resources on growth areas

**Product Focus:**
- BMC Helix Operations Management with Discovery for AIOps
- BMC Helix ITSM with BMC HelixGPT
- Improved employee experience and productivity

**Implication for SolarWinds:** BMC Helix as an independent company may become more agile and competitive. Monitor for increased mid-market push.

---

### Ivanti

#### Status: No Major 2024-2025 Moves Identified

- No significant acquisitions announced
- Continued focus on unified ITSM + Endpoint + Security platform
- Maintained Gartner MQ Leader position

**Context:**
- Leadership connection to BMC (President previously led BMC Helix)
- 88 of Fortune 100 as customers
- 40,000+ total customers

**Implication for SolarWinds:** Ivanti appears focused on consolidation rather than expansion. Opportunity to compete on innovation velocity.

### Atlassian (Jira Service Management)

#### Rovo AI Launch (2024-2025)

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Product** | Atlassian Rovo (GenAI assistant) |
| **Foundation** | Atlassian Intelligence (with OpenAI) |
| **Availability** | Premium/Enterprise: Apr-Jul 2025; Standard: Aug-Oct 2025 |
| **Pricing** | Now included in all paid JSM subscriptions |

**Rovo Capabilities for JSM:**
- Agentic teammates for incident response
- Information from third-party sources via Teamwork Graph
- Root cause identification
- Automated workflows and request resolution
- One-click agent templates
- Knowledge-connected, context-aware responses
- Automated escalations

**Key Stat:** 88% of organizations already using AI for service management, 89% planning to increase investment (Atlassian State of AI report)

#### Recent Acquisitions

| Company | Value | Focus |
|---------|-------|-------|
| **Loom** | $975M | Asynchronous video |
| **Secoda** | Undisclosed | Data catalog for Rovo AI |

**Product Consolidation:**
- Jira Work Management and Jira Software consolidated
- Unified Jira platform strategy

**Implication for SolarWinds:** Atlassian bundling AI into existing subscriptions removes friction. Compete on non-Atlassian independence and ESM for non-dev teams.

## Competitive Trends Analysis

### Dominant Theme: AI Arms Race

| Vendor | AI Investment Level | Approach |
|--------|--------------------| ---------|
| **ServiceNow** | ★★★★★ | Acquisition-led (Moveworks + tuck-ins) |
| **Freshworks** | ★★★★☆ | Organic (Freddy) + M&A war chest |
| **Atlassian** | ★★★★☆ | Platform (Rovo) + acquisition (Secoda) |
| **BMC Helix** | ★★★☆☆ | HelixGPT organic development |
| **ManageEngine** | ★★★☆☆ | Zoho ecosystem (Zia) |
| **Ivanti** | ★★☆☆☆ | Limited visible investment |
| **SolarWinds SD** | ★★★☆☆ | GenAI features (Premier) |

### Key Strategic Patterns

| Pattern | Vendors | Implication |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| **AI bundling** | Atlassian, ManageEngine | AI becoming table stakes, not premium |
| **M&A acceleration** | ServiceNow, Freshworks | Larger players buying capabilities |
| **Corporate restructuring** | BMC | Focus enabling faster innovation |
| **Vertical targeting** | Freshworks (healthcare/finance) | Specialized positioning |
| **Platform consolidation** | Atlassian, ServiceNow | Single-vendor preference growing |
