# Question 3: What are the major trends shaping the market?

## Answer

Five major trends are reshaping the ITSM market: AI/ML integration, cloud-native platforms, Experience Level Agreements (XLAs), Enterprise Service Management (ESM) expansion, and low-code/no-code development. Each trend creates both opportunities and competitive pressure.

---

## 1. AI/ML Integration

### Market Reality
- **44%** of new ITSM implementations in 2024-2025 included AI/ML modules for automated incident triage
- **38%** of organizations use conversational AI for first-line service desk interactions
- **42%** of enterprise buyers increased ITSM budgets for automation projects in 2024-2025
- **30%** allocated funds specifically for AI/ML pilots
- By 2026, the **"AI-first service desk"** will be the reference model

### Measured Impact
| Metric | Improvement |
|--------|-------------|
| Ticket volume reduction (self-service) | 20-40% |
| Agent efficiency improvement | 15-25% |
| Average handle time reduction | 20% |
| Response time improvement | 58% of firms report gains |

### AI Capability Tiers Emerging
| Tier | Capabilities | Market Position |
|------|--------------|-----------------|
| **AI-Native** | AI mediates every interaction; autonomous resolution | Leaders (2026+) |
| **AI-Augmented** | GenAI suggestions, summaries, routing | Current competitive requirement |
| **AI-Enabled** | Basic ML for classification, KB suggestions | Table stakes |
| **No AI** | Manual processes only | Becoming uncompetitive |

### SolarWinds Service Desk Alignment

| Capability | Status | Assessment |
|------------|--------|------------|
| Traditional AI (routing, classification) | ✓ Available | AI-Enabled tier |
| GenAI suggested solutions | ✓ Premier | AI-Augmented tier |

| Virtual Agent | ✓ Available | Needs capability verification |
| Autonomous resolution | ? Unknown | Gap vs. AI-Native leaders |

**Strategic Position:** Currently AI-Augmented (competitive), but market moving toward AI-Native by 2026.

## 3. XLA vs SLA (Experience Level Agreements)

### The Shift from SLAs to XLAs
| Aspect | SLA (Traditional) | XLA (Emerging) |
|--------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Focus** | Technical metrics | User experience |
| **Measures** | Uptime, response time, availability | Ease of use, satisfaction, productivity impact |
| **Perspective** | IT-centric | Human-centric |
| **Risk** | "Watermelon effect" — green metrics, red experience | Requires new measurement capabilities |

### The "Watermelon Effect"
Traditional SLA-focused service desks risk being "watermelons" — green on the outside (all KPIs met) but red on the inside (poor user experience and employee resentment). XLAs address this by measuring actual outcomes.

### Industry 5.0 Context
The transition from Industry 4.0 to Industry 5.0 emphasizes:
- **Resilience** over pure efficiency
- **Human-centricity** over process optimization
- **Value co-creation** over service delivery

XLAs align with this shift — human-centric IT services are becoming the benchmark for success.

### Best Practice: XLAs + SLAs Together
- XLAs complement SLAs, not replace them
- SLAs provide foundational data and service definitions
- XLAs add experience measurement layer
- Combined approach provides 360-degree view of technical and user experience metrics

## 4. Enterprise Service Management (ESM) Expansion

### Market Reality
- **50%** of IT teams now extend ITSM practices to non-IT departments
- ESM expands ITSM to HR, Facilities, Legal, Finance, and other business functions
- By 2026, ESM platforms will act as **orchestration layers** across departmental tools
- AI-driven routing and knowledge reuse across domains emerging

### ESM Value Proposition
| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Unified employee portal | Single entry point for all company services |
| Consistent service delivery | Same SLA/workflow discipline across departments |
| Knowledge leverage | Reuse resolution patterns across domains |
| Process standardization | Shared automation and governance |

### ESM Maturity Levels
| Level | Description | Capabilities |
|-------|-------------|--------------|
| **Level 1** | IT-only ITSM | Traditional IT service desk |
| **Level 2** | Departmental pilots | HR or Facilities using ITSM tools |
| **Level 3** | Multi-department ESM | Unified platform, siloed data |
| **Level 4** | Orchestrated ESM | AI routing across domains, shared knowledge |

### SolarWinds Service Desk Alignment

| Capability | Status | Assessment |
|------------|--------|------------|
| ESM mode | ✓ Advanced/Premier | Full capability |
| Pre-built templates (HR, Facilities, Legal) | ✓ Yes | Competitive |
| Data isolation between providers | ✓ Yes | Enterprise requirement met |
| Shared forms & automation | ✓ Yes | Reduces setup burden |
| Cross-provider AI routing | ? Unknown | Potential Level 4 gap |
| Unified employee portal | ✓ Yes | "Employee Center" UX |

**Strategic Position:** Strong ESM capabilities (Level 3). Well-positioned for the 50% of IT teams extending ITSM beyond IT. See `product/capabilities/esm.md` for details.

## 5. Low-Code/No-Code Development

### Market Reality
- **70%** of new applications expected to be built using low-code/no-code by 2025 (up from 25% in 2020)
- Low-code platform market: $28.75B (2024) → $264.40B (2032) at **32.2% CAGR**
- LCNC is **augmenting, not replacing** traditional ITSM
- "Citizen development movement" empowering non-developers to automate tasks

### Driving Factors
| Driver | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| Business agility | Workflows must evolve without developer backlogs |
| IT resource constraints | Reduce reliance on external consultancies |
| Time-to-value | Faster rollout of new service processes |
| Democratization | ITSM admins (not just developers) can build workflows |

### LCNC ITSM Use Cases
1. **Incident triage workflows** — Auto-route, escalate, notify
2. **Change approval processes** — Multi-step workflows with dynamic routing
3. **Service catalog items** — Custom request forms and fulfillment
4. **Integration orchestration** — Connect ITSM to other business systems
5. **Reporting/dashboards** — Custom analytics without development

### Competitive Landscape
| Vendor | LCNC Approach |
|--------|---------------|
| ServiceNow | App Engine, Flow Designer (extensive) |
| Freshservice | Workflow Automator |
| Jira Service Management | Automation rules, Forge apps |
| HCL BigFix | No-code, Agentic AI-powered |
| Xurrent | Low-code configuration |

| Custom forms | ✓ Yes | Available |

## Trend Convergence: Hyperautomation

The five trends are converging into **hyperautomation** — combining AI, RPA, ML, low-code, and intelligent decision engines:

```
AI/ML + Low-Code + Cloud-Native + ESM + XLA Focus
                    ↓
            Hyperautomation
                    ↓
    Autonomous, Experience-Centric Service Delivery
```

By 2026, leading ITSM platforms will offer:
- AI-native service desks (every interaction AI-mediated)
- ESM orchestration across departments
- XLA-driven experience measurement
- Citizen-developer workflow creation
- Cloud-native elastic infrastructure
