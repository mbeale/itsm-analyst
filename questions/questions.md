## Product Knowledge

### Current Capabilities
16. What is the complete feature inventory of the platform? → [Answer](question_16.md)
17. Where does the product excel relative to competitors?
18. Where are the critical feature gaps?
19. What is the platform's differentiated value proposition?

### Technical Architecture
20. Is the platform cloud-native, hybrid, or legacy?
21. What is the integration architecture (APIs, pre-built connectors, iPaaS)?
22. How extensible is the platform for custom workflows and apps?
23. What technical debt exists that constrains strategic options?
24. What is the current scalability ceiling?

### Customer Segmentation
25. Which verticals have the highest penetration and win rates?
26. Which company sizes (SMB, mid-market, enterprise) are best served?
27. Which use cases drive the most value for customers?
28. Where does the product struggle to compete?
29. What does win/loss analysis reveal by segment?

## Financial & Operational Data

### Unit Economics
30. What is customer acquisition cost (CAC) by segment and channel?
31. What is customer lifetime value (LTV) by segment?
32. What is the LTV:CAC ratio?
33. What are gross and net revenue retention rates?
34. What are churn rates and primary churn drivers?
35. What is expansion revenue as a percentage of total ARR growth?

### Pricing & Packaging
36. What is the current pricing model (per user, per ticket, tiered)?
37. How does pricing compare to market (premium, parity, discount)?
38. What is the average deal size by segment?
39. What flexibility exists for strategic or competitive deals?
40. Are there packaging gaps that competitors exploit?

## Data Access Requirements

### Systems to Access
- CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot) for pipeline and win/loss data
- Product analytics (Pendo, Amplitude, Mixpanel) for usage data
- Support platform (own product, Zendesk) for ticket trends
- Finance system for revenue and unit economics
- Competitive intelligence tools (Klue, Crayon)
- Market research (Gartner, Forrester, IDC reports)

### Regular Reports Needed
- Monthly ARR and pipeline report
- Quarterly win/loss analysis
- Quarterly competitive landscape update
- Annual market sizing and trend analysis
- Product usage and adoption metrics

- Product leadership (roadmap alignment)

- Customer success (retention drivers, expansion opportunities)
- Finance (unit economics, investment cases)
- Engineering (technical feasibility, architecture constraints)
- Marketing (positioning, demand generation effectiveness)
- Executive team and board (strategic alignment)
