# Question 10: Where are competitors investing R&D resources?

## Answer

The overwhelming R&D focus across all ITSM competitors is **Agentic AI** — autonomous AI agents that can execute tasks, make decisions, and resolve issues without human intervention. This represents a fundamental shift from "AI as assistant" to "AI as autonomous worker."

---

## Executive Summary

| Vendor | R&D Spend | Primary Investment Areas |
|--------|-----------|-------------------------|
| **ServiceNow** | $2.54B (23% of revenue) | Agentic AI, Now Assist, AI Agent Studio |
| **BMC Helix** | Not disclosed | HelixGPT, Agent Builder, AI Guardian |
| **Ivanti** | Not disclosed | AI diagnostics, incident correlation |
| **Freshservice** | Not disclosed | Freddy Agentic AI, DEX, acquisitions |
| **Jira SM** | Part of Atlassian R&D | Rovo Agents, Virtual Agent, Teamwork Graph |
| **ManageEngine** | Not disclosed | Zia Agents, Agent Studio, Zia LLM |

**Key Theme:** 2025 marks the shift from "chatting with AI" to "delegating to AI" — every major vendor is racing to build autonomous agents.

## Tier-1 Competitor R&D Analysis

### 1. ServiceNow — The R&D Leader

#### R&D Investment Scale

| Year | R&D Spend | YoY Growth | % of Revenue |
|------|-----------|------------|--------------|
| 2022 | $1.77B | — | — |
| 2023 | $2.12B | +20.1% | — |
| 2024 | $2.54B | +19.7% | 23% |

ServiceNow's R&D budget ($2.54B) exceeds the **total revenue** of most ITSM competitors combined.

#### Primary Investment Areas

**1. Agentic AI (Highest Priority)**
- AI agents that autonomously resolve IT outages and HR requests
- "Xanadu" and "Yokohama" platform releases introduced fully autonomous agents
- Target: $10B in AI-related ACV by 2025 (currently approaching $500M milestone)
- 150% quarter-over-quarter growth in AI deals
- 1,000+ customers already using AI features

**2. AI Agent Studio & Governance**
- AI Agent Studio: No-code tool for creating custom AI agents
- AI Control Tower: Governance, security, and monitoring of AI agents
- "Agentic AI orchestration" across front, middle, and back office

**3. GenAI / Now Assist**
- Now Assist: GenAI capabilities embedded throughout platform
- Bring-your-own-LLM capability
- Partnership with Nvidia for custom LLM development
- Industry-specific GenAI models in development

**4. Strategic Acquisitions**
- **Moveworks** (2024): Conversational AI for employee service
- Focus on acquiring AI-native capabilities vs. building from scratch

#### Strategic Implications

| Signal | Implication for SolarWinds |
|--------|---------------------------|
| 23% of revenue on R&D | Impossible to match; must be selective |
| $10B AI ACV target | AI becoming core monetization strategy |
| Agent Studio (no-code) | Democratizing AI agent creation |
| Moveworks acquisition | Consolidating AI talent through M&A |

**3. AI Governance (AI Guardian)**

- Operational cost/value metrics
- Risk analytics within observability dashboards

**4. Strategic Partnerships & Acquisitions**
- **March 2025**: Acquired AI automation startup (accelerate agentic AI)
- **May 2025**: Microsoft partnership to embed Helix AI in Azure services

#### Market Recognition

BMC Helix received highest scores possible in Forrester Wave (Q4 2025) for:
- Vision and roadmap
- Agentic AI development and orchestration
- Innovation
- Analytics/dashboards

## Tier-2 Competitor R&D Analysis

### 4. Freshworks/Freshservice — Aggressive AI Pivot

#### Strategic Priority

CEO Dennis Woodside stated Freshworks is **"doubling down"** on Employee Experience (EX), which includes ITSM, ITAM, ITOM, and ESM.

#### R&D Investments

**1. Freddy Agentic AI Platform (Flagship)**
- Launched Q2 2025
- **$20M ARR within months of launch** (combined with Freshservice Journeys)
- AI-related ARR more than doubled year-over-year
- Target: 40% Freddy AI adoption across customer base by 2026
- Industry-specific solutions for healthcare and finance in development

**2. Freddy AI Capabilities**
- AI Agents driving up to 50% ticket resolution rates
- AI-driven ticket assignment (workload/availability/skillset)
- Freddy AI Copilot: 76.6% decrease in resolution time, 41.1% improvement in first response
- Conversational analytics (Freddy AI Insights)
- Integration with Google Drive, Microsoft 365 Copilot

**3. Acquisitions Supporting AI Strategy**
- **Device42** ($230M, 2024): Cloud asset management
- **FireHydrant** (late 2025): Incident response

#### Financial Context

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| FY 2024 Revenue | $720.4M (+20.8% YoY) |
| FY 2025 Guidance | ~$825M (+14-15%) |
| Freshservice for Business Teams ARR | $35M+ (doubled in past year) |

### 5. Atlassian/Jira Service Management — Platform-Wide AI

#### Strategic Direction

> "2025 marks a major turning point for Atlassian. The company is rebuilding its Cloud platform to make teamwork smarter, faster, and more automated, with AI at the core."

Atlassian is shifting **all development resources to Cloud** for faster feature releases.

#### R&D Investments

**1. Rovo Agents (Agentic AI)**
- Launched October 2024
- Intelligent assistants embedded in Rovo AI platform
- Aggregates insights from third-party sources
- Service mappings via Teamwork Graph for root cause analysis
- Automated playbook recommendations

**2. Virtual Service Agent**
- Multi-channel support (help center, Slack, Teams, email, widget)
- Intent flows and AI answers for ticket deflection
- Premium/Enterprise tier feature

**3. Atlassian Intelligence (Platform-Wide)**
- AI-powered ticket summarization
- Customer sentiment analysis (real-time)
- AI incident timeline (ML + GenAI)
- Suggested knowledge base topics (gap analysis)
- Audio briefings

**4. Teamwork Graph (Differentiator)**
- Connects people, goals, systems, and knowledge
- Powers contextual AI suggestions
- Unique asset from Atlassian's collaboration tool ecosystem

#### Cloud-First Investment

| Investment | Rationale |
|------------|-----------|
| All dev resources to Cloud | Faster releases, enhanced security |
| Data Center sunset path | Focus R&D on single platform |
| AI features Cloud-only | Incentivize cloud migration |

### 6. ManageEngine/Zoho — Proprietary AI Stack

#### Leadership Investment

**Sridhar Vembu (Zoho founder) transitioned to Chief Scientist** role to lead Zoho Labs and drive AI initiatives across all products, including ManageEngine.

#### R&D Philosophy

ManageEngine emphasizes building a **proprietary, end-to-end AI technology stack** vs. relying on third-party LLMs:
- Zia LLM (in-house) available at no additional cost
- Option to use ChatGPT or Azure OpenAI
- Focus on data security and privacy

#### R&D Investments

**1. Zia Agents Platform (February 2025)**
- Agent Studio: Build custom AI agents
- Agent Marketplace: Distribute and access pre-built agents
- Pre-built Zia Agents: IT Help Desk Agent, HR Agent, Customer Support Agent, etc.

**2. Zia AI Evolution (Decade-Long Investment)**

| Year | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| 2015 | Zia foundational AI launched |
| 2018 | Ask Zia conversational assistant |
| 2024 | Generative AI capabilities |
| 2025 | Agentic AI (Zia Agents) |

**3. ServiceDesk Plus GenAI (September 2025)**
- "Biggest generative AI release yet"
- Native AI capabilities across all editions at no additional cost
- Recognized in 2025 Gartner MQ for AI Applications in ITSM (Niche Player)

#### Competitive Differentiation

| Factor | ManageEngine Approach |
|--------|----------------------|
| AI cost | Included in standard pricing |
| LLM flexibility | Zia LLM or bring-your-own |
| Data privacy | Proprietary stack, no data sharing |
| Public roadmap | Transparent roadmap (rare in market) |

## Strategic Implications for SolarWinds

### Competitive Gaps

| Area | Gap Assessment |
|------|----------------|
| **R&D Scale** | ServiceNow spends ~300x SolarWinds estimate; can't compete on volume |
| **Agentic AI** | All competitors investing; SolarWinds positioning unclear |
| **No-Code AI Tools** | Competitors enabling business users to build agents |

### Strategic Options

| Option | Description | Risk Level |
|--------|-------------|------------|
| **Partner for AI** | Integrate third-party AI (OpenAI, Azure) vs. build | Low |
| **Acquire AI capability** | Buy AI-native startups | Medium |
| **Focus on niche AI** | Healthcare-specific AI, compliance-focused AI | Low |
| **Fast-follower** | Implement proven AI features vs. innovating | Low |

### Recommended Positioning

> "AI-enhanced ITSM with enterprise compliance" — Don't try to out-innovate ServiceNow on AI; instead, combine capable AI features with SolarWinds' HIPAA/compliance strengths.
