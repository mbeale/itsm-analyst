# Question 2: What is the projected market growth rate (CAGR) over the next 5 years?

## Answer

### Overall Market CAGR: 14-17% (2024-2030)

The ITSM market is projected to grow at a **14-17% CAGR** through 2030, with consensus around **14.4-15.9%** from major research firms.

| Research Firm | 2024 Value | 2030 Projection | CAGR |
|---------------|------------|-----------------|------|
| Grand View Research | $13.46B | $29.93B | 14.4% |
| Mordor Intelligence | $12.84B (2025) | $27.81B | 16.72% |
| Fortune Business Insights | $11.91B | $36.78B (2032) | 15.3% |
| MarketsandMarkets | $10.5B (2023) | $22.1B (2028) | 15.9% |
| Precedence Research | $13.49B | $51.93B (2034) | 14.43% |
| KBV Research | — | $24.5B | 13.3% |
| Apps Run The World | $11.4B | $15.4B (2029) | 6.2% |

**Note:** Apps Run The World uses a narrower software-only definition, resulting in lower CAGR.

### Growth Drivers

1. **AI/ML Integration** — 60% of enterprises using AI-driven ITSM; reduces incident resolution by ~50%
2. **Cloud Migration** — Cloud ITSM growing at 17.4% vs. 6.1% for on-prem
3. **ESM Expansion** — ITSM principles extending to HR, facilities, legal, finance
4. **Digital Transformation** — Post-pandemic acceleration of IT modernization
5. **Hybrid/Multicloud Management** — Need for unified management across environments

### Growth Inhibitors

1. **Large Enterprise Saturation** — Mature segment with slower growth
2. **Pricing Pressure** — SMB segment commoditizing (Freshservice, ManageEngine)
3. **Vendor Consolidation** — Platform players (ServiceNow, Atlassian) absorbing point solutions
