# Question 1: What is the current ITSM market size (TAM, SAM, SOM)?

### SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market): $6.5-8.4B

SAM is calculated by applying company-specific filters to TAM:

| Step | Filter | Market Size | Reduction |
|------|--------|-------------|-----------|
| 1 | TAM (Global ITSM) | $11-13B | — |
| 2 | Geographic (NA, EU, LATAM, APAC) | $10.7-13.7B | -$0.8-1.1B (MEA only) |
| 3 | Deployment (Cloud-only) | $7.0-8.9B | -$3.7-4.8B |
| 4 | Compliance (No FedRAMP) | $6.5-8.4B | -$400-500M |
| 5 | Segment (All) | $6.5-8.4B | $0 |
| 6 | Language (44 languages) | **$6.5-8.4B** | $0 |

#### Key SAM Exclusions
- **Geographic:** MEA only — AU data center serves all APAC (~$0.8-1.1B excluded)
- **Deployment:** On-premise requirements (~35% of market)
- **Compliance:** U.S. federal — no FedRAMP certification (~$400-500M)

#### SAM Enablers
- AU data center serves all APAC customers — full APAC coverage
- HIPAA with BAA signing — U.S. healthcare accessible (~$1.2-1.4B)
- 44 languages — full coverage for served regions
- All segments targeted — Enterprise, Mid-Market, SMB
