# Atlassian (Jira) Competitive Battlecard

Atlassian’s focus is on DevOps and the strong connection between Jira (Dev) and JSM (Ops).

**Product:** Jira Service Management (JSM)
**Website:** [https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira/service-management](https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira/service-management)
**Headquarters:** Sydney, Australia

## Why SolarWinds
SolarWinds Service Desk is not an adaptation of another platform into a Service Management platform, which means we have none of the restrictions, difficulties, or headaches of managing a platform not specifically design for ITSM teams. Our solution was built from the ground up for ITSM expert and by ITSM experts.

## How to Approach
Its vitally important to uncover how much the prospect loves/hates working in Jira and the importance of DevOps in their purchase process. If DevOps is their #1 focus and they love Jira, deprioritize the opportunity. Otherwise, focus on the purpose-built nature of Service Desk, that it’s not built on a clunky / cumbersome platform. If Data Residency is important, ensure the prospect asks Atlassian for a list of data included or not included.

## Why We Should Win
*   **Jira Shop:** A lot of companies do not like how Jira works and is administered.
*   **Data Residency:** Ours puts all data in the data center, some data for Atlassian is not stored locally (user account data).
*   **True ITSM tool:** Service Desk is focused 100% on IT operations
*   **Easier to manage:** No complex configuration or reliance on Jira experts.
*   **Predictable cost:** No need to license separate Atlassian products

## Why We Lose
*   **Jira Shop:** If the customer uses and loves Jira, they’re going to be happy with how JSM is structured
*   **Alert & On-Call Management:** OpsGenie is part of JSM (a stronger Squadcast integration will help with this)
*   **Price:** JSM is very well priced for everything included
*   **DevOps:** If this is the customer’s #1 goal we’ll struggle a bit with this.
*   **Customization-heavy buyers:** Jira appeals to buyers who want to build everything from scratch.

## Trap Setting
*   What are your Data Residency expectations? Which data do you need to have stored within the region? What happens if your user account data is not stored in your region? → **Jira’s data residency is often only available at higher tiers**
*   Most ITSM solutions are either purpose-built or adaptations of another product. Which one feels more important to you? → **JSM is an adaptation of a dev work management tool, not built ground-up for ITSM.**
*   How much time does your team spend maintaining Jira workflows or configurations? → **Expose admin overhead and reliance on technical staff**

## Objection Handling

**DevOps is important to us, how do you handle this?**
We integrate directly with Jira, so that tickets with included information can be sent directly to your engineers. They can continue working in a tool they love and your teams can work in a tool built precisely for them.

**We like the flexibility of Jira.**
Flexibility is powerful—but only if you have the resources. SolarWinds gives you pre-built best practices, but you can still customize with no code.

**Jira is cheaper.**
Upfront, maybe. But factor in the cost of additional tools (like Insight or Confluence), plus setup, consultants, and maintenance.
