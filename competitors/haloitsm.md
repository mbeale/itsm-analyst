# HaloITSM Competitive Battlecard

HaloITSM positions itself as a flexible and affordable solution designed for teams looking for a simple, easy-to-implement tool without the complexity of larger enterprise solutions.

**Product:** HaloITSM
**Website:** [https://usehalo.com/haloitsm](https://usehalo.com/haloitsm)
**Headquarters:** Stowmarket, Suffolk, United Kingdom

## Why SolarWinds
SolarWinds Service Desk excels in automation, scalability, and integration. While HaloITSM may appeal to cost-conscious users, it lacks the depth and flexibility offered by SolarWinds. Service Desk provides an intuitive interface, powerful workflows, and robust reporting, while seamlessly integrating with SolarWinds' broader IT suite for enhanced asset and network management. Additionally, SolarWinds offers stronger post-purchase support and a professional community.

## How to Approach
When positioning SolarWinds Service Desk against HaloITSM, focus on our ease of use, stronger automation capabilities, and deeper integration with other SolarWinds tools. Highlight the flexibility of Service Desk in adapting to various customer needs—whether they are looking to improve user experience or increase IT operational efficiency. Approach the conversation by first asking customers about their pain points with HaloITSM, especially around areas like service catalog management, automation, or reporting. Frame Service Desk as a solution that makes ITSM effortless, efficient, and scalable with minimal friction.

## Why We Should Win
*   **Superior Automation:** SolarWinds Service Desk provides more advanced automation features
*   **Integrated ITSM Suite:** Service Desk integrates seamlessly with SolarWinds' broader IT operations suite, making it easier to manage assets, configurations, and network performance, all from one place.
*   **Better Support & Community:** Customers benefit from an extensive support network and an active professional community that can help resolve issues quickly and share best practices.
*   **Less Complex Configurations:** Halo can be clunky when it comes to configurations and inferior in terms of the workflows feature.
*   **Enterprise Service Management:** While Halo does have ESM, it's not as comprehensive. They utilize a lighter touch of setting up departments without the full ability to segregate.
*   **Scalability:** Service Desk can scale with businesses more simply and quickly.

## Why We Lose
*   **Pricing Sensitivity:** HaloITSM may be perceived as a more budget-friendly option, which can make price-sensitive customers lean towards it.
*   **Feature Complexity:** Some customers may find the broader set of features in SolarWinds Service Desk to be overwhelming compared to HaloITSM's simpler, more straightforward approach, especially if they’re looking for a lightweight solution with fewer bells and whistles.
*   **Available Features:** Project management, shift management with calendars, major incidents
*   **Deployment Services:** Have good ITSM partners for consultancy and deployment (e.g. Pink Elephant)

## Trap Setting
*   What are your Data Residency expectations? Which data do you need to have stored within the region? What happens if your user account data is not stored in your region? → **HaloITSM is not explicit where data is stored and they indicate data may be transferred to the UK or other countries they operate.**
*   How much time does your team spend maintaining workflows or configurations? → **HaloITSM can require extensive customization to meet unique business needs**
*   Would integrating other tools like assets, configuration, or network performance with your ITSM be a helpful simplification? → **HaloITSM often lacks deep integration with other IT management tools, leading to siloed operations and inefficiencies.**

## Objection Handling

**HaloITSM seems more affordable?**
I get it—price matters. But with SolarWinds Service Desk, you're investing in long-term value. Our automation, integration with other SolarWinds tools, and reduced admin work can save your team time and money as your needs grow. It's about efficiency now and down the road.

**HaloITSM feels simpler to implement, and we don't need a complicated solution. Is Service Desk overkill?**
I hear you. SolarWinds Service Desk is flexible—you can keep it simple and tailor it to what you need today, with room to grow. It's user-friendly and quick to set up, so you get the right tools without any extra complexity.
