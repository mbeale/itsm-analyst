# NinjaOne Competitive Battlecard

NinjaOne is a unified IT management platform that helps IT teams remotely monitor, manage, and support endpoints

**Product:** IT management
**Website:** [https://www.ninjaone.com/itsm/](https://www.ninjaone.com/itsm/)
**Headquarters:** Austin, Texas, USA

## Why SolarWinds
NinjaOne, which is primarily a remote monitoring and management (RMM) tool, SolarWinds Service Desk is specifically designed for ITSM, offering a comprehensive set of features such as SLA management, knowledge base, self-service portals, and change management—all critical for organizations looking for a full-fledged ITSM solution.

## How to Approach
When positioning SolarWinds Service Desk against NinjaOne, focus on its holistic ITSM capabilities, emphasizing the need for more than just RMM tools. Highlight how ITSM encompasses a structured approach to managing IT services, with built-in features that help teams streamline workflows, improve customer service, and increase transparency. Acknowledge NinjaOne's strong RMM features but pivot the conversation toward how SolarWinds provides a more scalable and comprehensive solution for teams aiming to improve service delivery and automate processes across the organization. Focus on business needs like optimizing workflows, creating a more efficient service desk, and reducing downtime.

## Why We Should Win
*   **Complete ITSM Solution:** SolarWinds Service Desk covers the full ITSM lifecycle, including incident, change, problem, and service management, whereas NinjaOne is more of a point solution that excels at endpoint management but lacks deep ITSM functionality.
*   **Automation & Efficiency:** Service Desk automates workflows, ticket routing, and notifications, improving operational efficiency, while NinjaOne offers limited automation for ITSM processes.
*   **Better User Experience:** Service Desk’s self-service portal and knowledge base empower users to solve issues independently, reducing strain on service teams.
*   **Scalable:** SolarWinds Service Desk scales well with growing businesses, while NinjaOne is ideal for smaller, less complex IT environments.

## Why We Lose
*   **Price Sensitivity:** Some customers might find NinjaOne more cost-effective, especially for smaller teams that primarily need RMM features without requiring full ITSM capabilities.
*   **RMM-Centric Focus:** For companies heavily invested in RMM as their primary tool for managing endpoints, NinjaOne could be perceived as a more specialized solution.
*   **Smaller Teams:** Teams with less complexity and fewer service management needs may not immediately see the need for the full breadth of SolarWinds Service Desk.

## Trap Setting
*   How are you currently handling service requests, incidents, and changes within your team?
*   Are there any pain points or inefficiencies you're encountering when managing tickets or service requests?
*   Do you feel your current solution is providing you with enough visibility into IT service performance and areas for improvement?
*   → **Based on the answers, guide the conversation toward SolarWinds Service Desk’s strengths in providing a complete ITSM solution. Focus on its automation, reporting, and ease of integration.**

## Objection Handling

**It seems like SolarWinds Service Desk might be overkill for our needs.**
I understand that, but even for small or mid-sized teams, having a structured system in place can lead to huge efficiency gains. SolarWinds Service Desk can scale with you—offering basic functionalities at first and then growing with your needs as your team expands. It’s not just a tool for today; it’s an investment in the future.

**We’re concerned about the cost difference between NinjaOne and SolarWinds.**
It’s true that SolarWinds Service Desk comes with more robust features, but the value you get in return—especially with the added capabilities like asset management, change management, and powerful reporting—far outweighs the initial cost difference. Plus, with the automation it provides, you’ll save time and money in the long run.
