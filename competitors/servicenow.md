# ServiceNow Competitive Battlecard

ServiceNow is a leading, enterprise-focused platform known for its extensive customization options and complex configurations, often favored by large organizations with sophisticated IT needs.

**Product:** IT Service Management
**Website:** [https://www.servicenow.com/products/itsm.html](https://www.servicenow.com/products/itsm.html)
**Headquarters:** Santa Clara, California, USA

## Why SolarWinds
ServiceNow is often overkill for many organizations, with its complex configurations, long implementation times, and high costs. SolarWinds Service Desk simplifies ITSM by offering a user-friendly, cost-effective solution that can be quickly deployed with minimal customization. It provides all the essential features without the complexity and expense associated with ServiceNow, making it ideal for businesses looking for efficiency and scalability.

## How to Approach
Focus on complexity and high costs associated with ServiceNow. Ask them about their priorities: Do they need something that works out of the box? Are they overwhelmed with complex configurations or high implementation costs? Emphasize that Service Desk is designed to be an affordable, flexible alternative to ServiceNow—ideal for organizations seeking a simple yet powerful ITSM solution. Highlight the ability to get up and running quickly without heavy reliance on consultants or developers.

## Why We Should Win
*   **Simplicity and Speed:** Service Desk offers a much faster and simpler implementation process, reducing the time-to-value.
*   **Cost-Effectiveness:** Service Desk has transparent and predictable pricing, and is significantly more affordable without the need for specialist employees to configure
*   **User Experience:** SolarWinds is designed with the end-user in mind, offering an intuitive interface and easy navigation
*   **Scalability:** Service Desk can scale with businesses without the need for complex configurations.
*   **Support and Customer Success:** Service Desk tech support is 24/7. In addition, customers have access to dedicated resources like AEs/CSMs, helping ensure real people will join calls to support.

## Why We Lose
*   **Brand Recognition:** ServiceNow is a well-established name in the ITSM market, which can make it more appealing to larger enterprises that are looking for a widely recognized solution.
*   **Enterprise-Focused Features:** ServiceNow may offer more advanced capabilities in certain high-demand, complex enterprise environments, which can appeal to large organizations with extensive IT requirements.
*   **Customization:** While Service Desk is simple to use, some organizations that require heavy customization may lean toward ServiceNow.

## Trap Setting
*   What’s the typical timeline for implementing your ITSM solution? How quickly do you need changes or new features implemented? → **ServiceNow often requires lengthy implementation cycles, with changes needing customization and a dedicated team to manage them.**
*   How flexible do you need your ITSM solution to be in adapting to your unique business processes? What happens if you need to make a change or tweak workflows after deployment? → **ServiceNow can be rigid and difficult to optimize for specific business needs without requiring complex, expensive customizations.**
*   What’s your budget for an ITSM solution? How much of that budget is allocated to ongoing maintenance and support? → **ServiceNow’s pricing structure often involves high upfront costs and expensive ongoing maintenance, making it less cost-effective for mid-sized businesses compared to SolarWinds Service Desk.**

## Objection Handling

**ServiceNow is the gold standard.**
Totally get that—ServiceNow is a recognized name. But in our experience, that recognition often comes with high costs and long deployment cycles. What I’d like to show you is how we can match their core capabilities like CMDB and ITIL workflows, while getting you live faster and without the overhead of a complex modular setup.

**We’ve been burned by generic ‘me too’ ITSM vendors.**
I hear that a lot—there are so many ITSM tools out there that sound the same. What I try to do differently is start with your goals, not just a product demo. My job isn’t to sell you a license—it’s to help you build the right roadmap.

**We’re concerned about long-term scalability.**
I appreciate you thinking long-term—that’s actually where we shine. Our platform doesn’t just handle service management today; we’re aligned with your broader IT strategy. Whether it’s adding observability, network monitoring, or scaling ITAM processes, we’re built to grow with you.
