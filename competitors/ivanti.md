## Why SolarWinds
Unlike Ivanti, which focuses on complex and enterprise-heavy solutions that can overwhelm teams with unnecessary features, SolarWinds Service Desk provides a streamlined, intuitive experience that balances robust functionality with ease of use. While Ivanti may require significant resources to implement and manage, SolarWinds ensures a quick, low-friction deployment with a solution that scales effortlessly as your organization grows. SolarWinds delivers all the power you need for IT service management without the complexity, offering a flexible solution that puts user experience first.

## How to Approach
When engaging with prospects considering Ivanti Neurons, focus on emphasizing SolarWinds' simplicity and user experience. Ask open-ended questions to uncover pain points in their current solution, particularly in terms of user adoption, implementation complexity, or scalability challenges. Stress the ease of getting started and adoption with SolarWinds Service Desk and highlight how it can drive efficiencies without overwhelming service teams. Position SolarWinds Service Desk as a solution that reduces operational complexity and accelerates value, with straightforward setup and ongoing support.

## Why We Should Win
*   **Simplicity and Ease of Use:** SolarWinds Service Desk’s intuitive interface allows users to get started quickly, reducing training time and increasing user adoption.
*   **Transparent Pricing:** SolarWinds delivers a more predictable, all-inclusive pricing model that scales as the organization does
*   **Quick Time to Value:** The solution is designed to implement swiftly, with minimal disruption, helping customers realize ROI faster.
*   **Strong Customer Support and Community:** SolarWinds’ reputation for responsive customer support and its active user community sets it apart in the market.

## Why We Lose
*   **Larger Enterprises or Complex Needs:** Ivanti Neurons may be favored by larger enterprises with highly complex IT environments requiring more advanced IT operations and automation capabilities.
*   **Established Relationships:** Organizations that already have a deep relationship with Ivanti may hesitate to switch due to perceived implementation risks or fear of disruption.
*   **Cherwell EOL:** Some customers may be moving automatically to Ivanti due to their acquisition of Cherwell and feel an additional change may be too much
