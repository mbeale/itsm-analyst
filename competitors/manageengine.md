# ManageEngine Competitive Battlecard

ManageEngine is a strong ”tools” company, having a giant portfolio of various IT tools.

**Product:** ServiceDesk Plus
**Website:** [https://www.manageengine.com/products/service-desk/](https://www.manageengine.com/products/service-desk/)
**Headquarters:** Pleasanton, California, USA

## Why SolarWinds
Not only is Service Desk is built for fast time-to-value, intuitive user experiences, and cost-efficiency, but SolarWinds has a much more focused portfolio with a lot of investment focused on our ITSM solution. Being a customer is more than just purchasing a tool. You feel as a true partner with great customer support, deployment help, a large IT community base, community events, and no surprise fees.

## How to Approach
Encourage the prospect to truly understand what their year 2+ costs are going to be from ManageEngine, including future add-ons that are included all-in with us. Highlight what it means to be a SolarWinds customer beyond just buying a product (see above). Really showcase our support, customer service, THWACK community, quarterly roadmap updates, and deployment options. Emphasize the ease of administration and intuitive nature of our product.

## Why We Should Win
*   **Post-purchase experience:** Support, deployment, THWACK; customers realize they need guidance
*   **ITSM & ITIL Focused:** Built from day 1 for ITSM
*   **Easy to administer and use:** Built by ITIL/ITSM experts that understand how ITSM teams actually work. SolarWinds offers simplicity without sacrificing capability.

## Why We Lose
*   **Pricing:** ManageEngine heavily heavily discounts the first year or two to get the sale, likely at a loss
*   **Value:** Customers do not see the value of post-purchase as important enough for the higher year 1 cost
*   **Local Partners:** International buyers prefer to have local deployment partners

## Trap Setting
*   How are you thinking about implementation and support? What’s important to you? → **ManageEngine falls short on heavy admin overhead, slow setup, need for pro services**
*   What do your budget cycles typically look like? Is it important to have consistent pricing year to year on your purchases? → **no surprise add-ons from SolarWinds**
*   How important is it to have an all-in solution from the start, versus having a bunch of extra modules for you to purchase? → **See if they show frustration with complex or piecemeal systems**
*   How much time do you have to devote to administering your solution? Is an easy-to-navigate and intuitive interface important?

## Objection Handling

**Why are you more expensive?**
This is just part of ManageEngine’s general go-to-market strategy, that they heavily discount you on year 1 to get in the door. Then at your renewal, you get hit with the full price list. There are also a lot of extra add-ons required that add-up to a higher total cost. We include all of this from day 1, plus excellent post-product support.

**Do you have a local partner that can help deployment for us?**
We have a number of partners around the world and we’re continuing to grow that base. If you have a partner that you’re comfortable working with, I can see if they’re in our program. Otherwise, we can reach out to them, and work with them to get you going. That way you’re more comfortable with someone local to you.

**ManageEngine gives us more customization.**
SolarWinds focuses on configurability without complexity. Most customers don’t need to code or script to automate and optimize processes—and that means faster onboarding, easier maintenance, and more agile operations.
