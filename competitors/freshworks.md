# Freshworks Competitive Battlecard

Freshworks got their start as a CRM solution and has expanded into other areas such as ITSM.

**Product:** Freshservice; Freshdesk
**Website:** [https://freshservice.com](https://freshservice.com)
**Headquarters:** San Mateo, California, USA

## Why SolarWinds
SolarWinds Service Desk was purpose built, from the ground up, for ITSM teams by ITIL experts. It is not an adoption of a non-ITSM platform. We’ve built our solution and packaging to align perfectly to grow with your business as your practices mature.

## How to Approach
Frame the conversation around value, depth, and simplicity. Freshworks also has different pricing/packaging than us, putting less important features in their lower tiers, and more important features in their highest tiers (like audits) or as add-ons. Try to spend more time focusing on CMDB, Asset Discovery, AI, Virtual Agent, and Audits because those features are in Fresh’s highest tiers or extra-cost add-ons. Focus on post-purchase experience with us and encourage them to research what this looks like as a Fresh customer too. Ask prospects whether they want to grow IT maturity without stacking up costs and tools. This resonates with IT leaders watching both budgets and tool sprawl.

## Why We Should Win
*   **Full ITSM suite included:** AI ticketing, CMDB, asset discovery, automation, and service catalog come standard in one or more of the plans, not as add-ons
*   **No hidden limitations:** API calls and automations are unlimited with us
*   **ITSM & AIOps:** Observability integration if this is important to prospects
*   **Purpose-built:** Designed for IT, not a generalist platform. SolarWinds serves IT pros
*   **Ease of Use:** Simpler setup and faster time-to-value
*   **Available support:** Fresh may not have account managers in same time zone and only offer 24/5 phone support, leaving a large person-to-person support gap for real-time help

## Why We Lose
*   **Value & Price:** If prospect does not believe the full value of SWSD they may go with Freshworks
*   **Project Management:** Companies that want full project management capabilities are better suited for Freshworks
*   **Shows Well:** Freshworks demos really well. Freshservice has a polished UI that can initially impress, especially with non-technical buyers. Customer particularly love OOTB integrations, workflow and major incident creation capabilities. If the prospect doesn’t pick it apart, they won’t see how cumbersome it is.

## Trap Setting
*   How do you manage automatic discovery of CMDB items and visually mapping them? → **This is an add-on capability for Fresh**
*   Are audits a requirement for your business? To see any changes, accesses, etc?
*   How many automation workflows / orchestrations are you planning to run? → **Fresh limits automation on lower tiers.**
*   How much time do you have to devote to administering your solution? Is an easy-to-navigate and intuitive interface important? → **Fresh can be more complex to setup and administer, creating more loss of time**
*   How are you thinking about implementation and support? What’s important to you? → **SolarWinds excels at post-purchase support**

## Objection Handling

**How come you don’t have Project Management?**
This is a capability we are actively working on and making some additions to the product in 2025 with. Historically most customers have been working in applications dedicated to Project Management and so don’t really look for ITSM to handle that. However, this is changing as customers consolidate tools, so we’re meeting that demand soon.

**How come you’re more expensive?**
Actually, when you compare total cost for the features IT teams *actually need*, we’re often the better value. With Freshworks, many critical ITSM features—like AI ticketing, CMDB visualization, asset discovery, audit trails, and advanced automation—are only available in higher tiers or sold as add-ons. That means what starts as a lower price quickly grows. SolarWinds includes those capabilities in standard tiers, so you’re not paying extra to do what should be core ITSM. You get a complete, scalable platform from day one—without having to upgrade or bolt on essential tools later.
