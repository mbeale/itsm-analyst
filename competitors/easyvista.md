# EasyVista Competitive Battlecard

EasyVista is generally regarded as a legacy ITSM provider with a strong presence in the enterprise market, offering highly customizable solutions for large organizations.

**Product:** EV Service Manager
**Website:** [https://www.easyvista.com/products/it-service-management-software/](https://www.easyvista.com/products/it-service-management-software/)
**Headquarters:** Noisy-le-Grande, France

## Why SolarWinds
SolarWinds Service Desk stands out with its intuitive and modern UI, offering a user experience that’s easy to navigate and reduces friction for both service teams and end users. This contrasts with EasyVista’s outdated UI, which can slow down adoption and frustrate users. Service Desk is built for simplicity, with powerful capabilities like advanced automation, reporting, and analytics to drive efficiency. Moreover, SolarWinds enables true citizen development with low or no-code capabilities, allowing service teams to customize workflows, forms, and integrations without relying on IT, which empowers teams to rapidly respond to business needs and scale efficiently.

## How to Approach
When positioning SolarWinds Service Desk against EasyVista, focus on highlighting the simplicity and agility of Service Desk. Emphasize how quickly teams can get up and running, particularly for organizations that want to reduce the complexity of their ITSM solutions. Position the easy integration with existing business systems, providing faster time-to-value. Highlight the user-friendly interface and the streamlined workflows that reduce friction, increase adoption, and ultimately lead to better employee satisfaction and service performance.

## Why We Should Win
*   **Ease of Use:** Service Desk has a cleaner, more intuitive UI with a quicker learning curve.
*   **Reporting & Analytics:** Out-of-the-box insights that drive smarter decision-making and proactive management.
*   **Customer Support:** SolarWinds offers more responsive, hands-on support.
*   **Cost-effectiveness:** More transparent and affordable pricing and better value for organizations trying to do more with less.
*   **ESM for Everyone (really):** Service Desk offers a more intuitive ESM solution that helps empower non-IT employees to create and manage their service provider.
*   **Knowledge Base:** Much more robust in Service Desk and Smart Suggestions are a standout as they guide users to solutions faster while helping deflect tickets.

## Why We Lose
*   **Customization:** EasyVista may offer deeper customization options for large, complex organizations with unique needs.
*   **Brand Recognition in ITSM:** In certain industries or geographies, especially Europe, EasyVista may have stronger brand recognition or established relationships with legacy clients.
*   **Cherwell > EasyVista Employees:** In North America, a very large portion of the sales organization is former Cherwell employees. They have a lot of street credibility with prospects, especially Cherwell customers.

## Trap Setting
*   Are you looking for a solution that allows non-IT teams to customize and adapt workflows easily? → **Highlight how SolarWinds’ low/no-code citizen development capabilities empower ESM teams without needing IT resources.**
*   How important is time-to-value for your organization? → **Highlight Service Desk’s quick setup and the ability to see immediate improvements in service delivery.**
*   Are you looking for a solution that integrates easily with your existing tools? → **SolarWinds Service Desk’s seamless integrations with other business systems. Service Desk provides out-of-the-box integrations and APIs to ensure data flows seamlessly across your organization.**

## Objection Handling

**EasyVista’s solution is more customizable for our unique needs.**
While EasyVista may offer deep customization, SolarWinds Service Desk strikes a balance between customization and simplicity. Its intuitive UI ensures quick adoption and efficiency, while the low/no-code capabilities enable you to make adjustments without involving IT, reducing the need for heavy development.

**EasyVista is more commonly used in our region, and we’re comfortable with the local support.**
While EasyVista may have a more established presence in certain regions, SolarWinds Service Desk provides exceptional global support through its active community, extensive documentation, and a dedicated team that’s ready to assist no matter where you’re located. Additionally, SolarWinds is known for its international reach, with resources and support in multiple languages, ensuring your organization gets the help it needs, no matter where you operate.
