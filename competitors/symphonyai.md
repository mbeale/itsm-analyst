# SymphonyAI Competitive Battlecard

SymphonyAI's broader focus is on AI applications across various industries, with an ITSM solution added to the portfolio in 2018.

**Product:** [Apex Platform] IT Service Management, IT Asset Management, Service Automation, Copilot
**Website:** [https://www.symphonyai.com/itsm/apex-platform/](https://www.symphonyai.com/itsm/apex-platform/)
**Headquarters:** Palo Alto, California, USA

## Why SolarWinds
Unlike SymphonyAI, which offers disparate solutions with a focus on automation and AI, SolarWinds takes a more holistic approach, offering ITSM, IT Asset Management, and Service Automation in one unified platform. SolarWinds Service Desk is built specifically for ITSM teams, focusing on the employee experience and the tools/features/setup that help reduce friction for ITSM teams.

## How to Approach
When speaking with prospects considering SymphonyAI, it’s important to highlight SolarWinds' ease of implementation and scalability across organizations of all sizes. Position SolarWinds Service Desk as the "all-in-one" solution that streamlines IT operations with minimal complexity, unlike SymphonyAI’s fragmented offering. Emphasize SolarWinds’ flexibility in tailoring ITSM workflows to meet the specific needs of the business while maintaining a user-friendly interface. Leverage SolarWinds’ reputation for customer support, community-driven enhancements, and a product roadmap that’s built on real-world feedback from IT teams.

## Why We Should Win
*   **Unified Platform:** SolarWinds Service Desk integrates IT Service Management, IT Asset Management, and Service Automation into one cohesive solution, eliminating the need for disparate tools and the complexity of managing multiple systems.
*   **Ease of Use:** For SymphonyAI, the user interface is functional, but could be more interactive to help new users navigate the system more easily.
*   **Scalability:** Symphony AI faces scalability challenges due to its fragmented approach that requires additional tools and lacks the flexibility and user-centric design needed for larger organizations.
*   **Focus on ITSM:** Unlike SymphonyAI, which integrates AI and automation in a way that may be disconnected from traditional ITSM processes, SolarWinds offers a full-featured ITSM solution that is directly aligned with the day-to-day needs of IT teams.

## Why We Lose
*   **Lack of AI-Centric Features:** SymphonyAI offers more sophisticated AI-driven capabilities through Copilot, which may appeal to customers looking for heavy AI automation in their ITSM solution.
*   **Automation Focus:** Customers who prioritize AI-powered automation over traditional ITSM functionality might gravitate toward SymphonyAI's more automation-heavy approach.
*   **Perception of Innovation:** SymphonyAI may be perceived as a more cutting-edge, AI-first solution, which could be an attractive selling point for certain tech-forward organizations.

## Trap Setting
*   How important is AI and automation in your ITSM needs? → **Acknowledge the importance of automation but highlight that SolarWinds offers practical automation that can integrate seamlessly into ITSM workflows.**
*   Are you looking for a full ITSM solution or just specific features like asset management or service automation? → **Emphasize that SolarWinds provides the full ITSM experience, including asset management, workflows, and service automation, all in one platform, allowing for better visibility and less fragmentation.**
*   Do you have concerns about managing multiple tools for different IT functions? → **Position SolarWinds as the comprehensive solution.**

## Objection Handling

While SymphonyAI offers advanced AI features, SolarWinds focuses on delivering practical and actionable automation that seamlessly integrates with your IT service workflows. This ensures that your team doesn’t get bogged down by complex setups, and it allows for more control over your processes.

**SymphonyAI seems more innovative and future-ready.**
Innovation is important, but it’s equally important to invest in a solution that’s proven to deliver results. SolarWinds has built a strong reputation in the ITSM space, continuously evolving our platform based on feedback from real users. We’re committed to helping you achieve your goals without introducing unnecessary complexity.
