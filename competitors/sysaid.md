# SysAid Competitive Battlecard

SysAid has positioned itself as a legacy ITSM provider with a focus on high customization, which can often lead to greater complexity.

**Products:** SysAid
**Website:** [https://www.sysaid.com/](https://www.sysaid.com/)
**Headquarters:** Tel Aviv, Israel

## Why SolarWinds
While SysAid struggles with clunky interfaces and outdated user experiences, SolarWinds offers a modern, intuitive solution designed for rapid implementation and maximum efficiency. Built to scale effortlessly from small businesses to large enterprises, SolarWinds is an all-in-one platform that doesn’t require complex customizations or costly add-ons, unlike SysAid.

## How to Approach
When engaging with prospects, take a strong stance on how SysAid has failed to evolve with the times. Many SysAid customers are stuck with a legacy platform that burdens their teams with unnecessary complexity. Position SolarWinds as the solution that delivers immediate value with minimal setup, while also improving efficiency through automation and integrations. Focus on how SolarWinds simplifies operations and reduces the overhead that SysAid customers are likely facing, offering them a clear path to a more streamlined, future-proof solution. SolarWinds offers a no-nonsense approach to ITSM—no fluff, just results.

## Why We Should Win
*   **Ease of Use:** SolarWinds’ intuitive interface is far simpler and faster to learn than SysAid’s outdated, clunky design.
*   **Scalability:** SolarWinds scales effortlessly without costly customizations, while SysAid’s complexity can delay deployment and increase costs and learning curves.
*   **Customer Support:** SysAid customers have experienced varying levels of support quality, with certain cases requiring multiple follow-ups.
*   **Transparent Pricing:** SolarWinds delivers a more predictable, all-inclusive pricing model, whereas SysAid hides additional costs for customizations and advanced features.

## Why We Lose
*   **Old Habits Die Hard:** Some prospects may be loyal to SysAid because they’ve spent years implementing it, and they might resist change.
*   **Perceived Customization Flexibility:** SysAid’s reputation for being highly customizable may sway prospects who are entrenched in their unique, niche needs. However, this customization comes at a price.
*   **Feature Overload Myth:** SysAid may tout more features, but those features are often bloated and not as efficient. Customers may get distracted by a long feature list but miss out on the streamlined, effective solution from SolarWinds.

## Trap Setting
*   What are your Data Residency expectations? Which data do you need to have stored within the region? What happens if your user account data is not stored in your region? → **SysAid has data residency limitations and is often only available at higher tiers**
*   Would this level of customization deliver the value you really need, or is it adding unnecessary complexity and overhead? → **SysAid’s customization can be appealing, but it often comes with hidden costs, additional maintenance, and a longer implementation cycle.**
*   How important is pricing transparency and knowing your total cost upfront for budgeting? → **SysAid’s pricing model can be unclear, with hidden costs for essential features and customizations.**

## Objection Handling

**SysAid has more features, and we need those.**
SysAid might have a long list of features, but many of those features are overly complex and not actually adding value. SolarWinds focuses on delivering exactly what you need with automation, ease of use, and scalability—all without the unnecessary bloat.

**We need a highly customizable solution, and SysAid offers that.**
Customization can be appealing, but it often leads to a more complicated, costly, and time-consuming implementation. SolarWinds Service Desk provides flexibility while being simple to configure and manage, ensuring a faster time to value without the burden of ongoing customization and maintenance.
